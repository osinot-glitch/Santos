Neymar Júnior

Neymar da Silva Santos Júnior (Brazilian Portuguese pronunciation: [nejˈmaʁ dɐ ˈsiwvɐ ˈsɐ̃tuz‿ˈʒuni.oʁ] ⓘ; born 5 February 1992) is a Brazilian professional footballer

who plays as an attacking midfielder or a forward for Campeonato Brasileiro Série A club Santos.

Known for his dribbling, technical ability, playmaking, and finishing, he is widely regarded as one of thegreatest players of all time.

He is the all-time top goalscorer of the Brazil national team, with 80 goals
